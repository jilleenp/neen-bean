About

Welcome to my corner of the world. I would tell you about myself, but

  1. I struggle to find easy categories to put myself into that I don't immediately follow with: "except...."
  2. I am pretty sure no one reads these kinds of pages anyway

All you need to know is that I am independent-minded. And that mind goes a lot of directions all at once. I have read that if you want people to read your work, you need to stick to a specific subject. That seems like very good advice. And it also sounds like advice that I will never, ever be able to follow. I have never been good at doing what I am told to do.

So here I will write about whatever I feel like writing about on any given day. Maybe some of it will be of interest to you.

If not, you are welcome to go build your own corner.

Three more things:

  • The name of this blog is curtesy of my Aunt Karyl. It was her nickname for me as a child. Aunt Karyl was my most vibrant relative in a family that is otherwise pretty down-to-earth. I miss her dearly.
  • I will use a lot of gratuitous photos on this blog, partly because I have them and partly because I enjoy using and looking at them. Some are photos I have taken, and others are photos taken by various family members. I have a lot of photos.
  • Speaking of Aunt Karyl and photos, Aunt Karyl took the photo on the homepage. I love my grandmother's roses, even if they are a little overgrown. And I love the smell of fog in a sleepy town in the valley.
